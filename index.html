Our story

From a family table to a polished coastal dining room

Marea Table began as a small kitchen built around family recipes, market ingredients, and the kind of hospitality that makes dinner feel unhurried.

Today, the restaurant brings together coastal flavors, precise technique, and a service rhythm designed for business dinners, anniversaries, and relaxed nights out.
